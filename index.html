    Data format

    Five tables, JSON (list of objects) or CSV (header row). JSON wins if both exist.

    patients
    id, name, age, condition, provider
    lab_orders
    id, patient_id, test, ordered_date, result_date, status (pending | resulted)
    prescriptions
    id, patient_id, drug, ordered_date, filled_date, status (ordered | filled)
    appointments
    id, patient_id, date, type, note, status (completed | scheduled | cancelled)
    messages
    id, patient_id, received_date, read (true | false), subject, body
    Rules

    Dates are ISO (YYYY-MM-DD). Stalled lab: pending, no result, ordered > 7 days ago. Unfilled prescription: ordered, not filled, > 3 days (high after 7). Overdue follow-up: a completed note says "follow up in N weeks" (digits or one–twelve), that date has passed, and there is no scheduled appointment today or later. Unanswered message: unread, received > 1 day ago. Extra columns are ignored. A single clinic.json holding all five tables also works.
